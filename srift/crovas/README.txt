Crovas is a bold neo-geometric sans serif typeface family designed for strong headlines, expressive layouts, and contemporary visual identities. Its distinctive geometric construction combines powerful heavy weights with refined lighter styles, creating a flexible typographic system that can shift from bold and impactful to clean and minimal.

The family includes 18 styles, featuring regular and oblique variations across multiple weights. This range makes Crovas suitable for both large display applications and supporting typography where a more restrained appearance is needed.

Crovas works particularly well for branding, visual identity, editorial design, posters, fashion graphics, sports campaigns, technology projects, packaging, advertising, social media graphics, websites, applications, and promotional materials.

The heavier styles are designed to create strong visual impact in headlines, logos, campaign graphics, and large-scale typography. The lighter weights provide a cleaner appearance for supporting information, layouts, captions, and modern editorial compositions.
